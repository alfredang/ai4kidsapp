# Changelog

All notable changes to the AI4Kids iOS/iPadOS app. The newest entry's bullets
are the source of truth for the App Store "What's New" notes.

## [1.6] — 2026-11-03 · build 10

### Added
- AI Workshops: Storybook Studio, Mini Maker Studio, Maths Detectives and Science Explorers, powered by DeepSeek.
- Grown-up login: a parent sets a PIN and adds their own DeepSeek API key before children can use the AI Workshops.
- Kid-safety filters keep personal details private, and sessions lock automatically.
- Parents' Corner can lock the AI Workshops, replace the key, change the PIN or turn AI off.

### Changed
- Warm cream colours and website-matched purple, teal and sunny-yellow styling.

### Privacy
- The seven play activities remain fully offline. Only text typed in an AI Workshop is sent to DeepSeek, using the parent's own key.
- App Store privacy label: "Data Not Linked to You → Other User Content (App Functionality)". Left the Kids Category (age band cleared).

### App Store submission
- 3 November 2026: 1.5 (build 9) was already approved and was released. Then 1.6 (build 10) was submitted and confirmed WAITING_FOR_REVIEW.

## [1.5] — 2026-11-03 · build 9

### Changed
- New sunny bookworm app icon and original colourful activity artwork, matching the AI Kids website.
- Sky-blue and lavender screens, scrapbook borders and larger, friendly Fraunces / Nunito typography.
- Original illustrated navigation and action icons throughout the activities.
- All seven learning activities remain fully offline; no account, ads or data collection added.

### App Store submission
- Submitted 3 November 2026: version 1.5, build 9, confirmed WAITING_FOR_REVIEW in App Store Connect.
- Five updated iPhone screenshots and four updated iPad screenshots; manual release after approval.

## [1.2] — 2026-07-21 · build 6

### Added
- Parental gate: a grown-ups-only math challenge now guards the Parents' Corner (which holds the progress reset), matching the Android app's Families Policy gate.

### App Store submission
- Submitted 2026-07-21. Fully offline, no data collection ("Data Not Collected" privacy label unchanged).

## [1.1] — 2026 · build 5 (released)

### Added
- Brain Arcade replaced Brain Games: eight quick solo card games (matching, memory, sums and sorting), fully offline.

## [1.0] — 2026 · builds 1–3

### Added
- Initial release: Phonics Playground, Story Builder, Code Puzzles, Brain Games; universal (iPhone + iPad); stars progress with Parents' Corner reset.
